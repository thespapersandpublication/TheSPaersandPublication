Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

At The S Papers and Publication, accessible from https://thespapersandpublication.com, protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by our website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

How We Use Your Information

Log Files

The S Papers and Publication follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected may include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks.
