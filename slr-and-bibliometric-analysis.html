Structured Literature Review & Scientific Mapping

At The S Papers and Publication, we help researchers build strong academic foundations through Systematic Literature Reviews (SLR) and Bibliometric Analysis.

Whether you are developing a thesis, framing a research gap, preparing a journal article, or advancing academic projects, our experts deliver structured, original, and evidence-based reviews.

Using tools like VOSviewer, R Studio, and Scopus datasets, we map key publications, authors, and research trends to uncover research fronts, collaboration patterns, and gaps worth exploring.
